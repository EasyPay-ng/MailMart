What MailMart notifies people about

Each switch below turns one event on or off for everyone. The two text boxes are the wording that appears in the bell and in the phone's notification shade. Nothing is sent until you save.

The bell is live while a MailMart page is open. Phone alerts are sent by the scheduled sender in .github/workflows/notify-cron.yml: it reads the records that changed since the last run, applies exactly these settings, and pushes to the free ntfy app — so they keep arriving with MailMart closed. That sender runs every five minutes, so treat phone alerts as “within a few minutes”, not instant. See “Notifications” in the README.
